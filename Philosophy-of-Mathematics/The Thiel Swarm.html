THE THIEL

HOW IT WORKS

1

Pulsar core

A young, fast-spinning neutron star. A Crab-like one sheds about 5×1031 W as it slows down, over 100,000 times the Sun's output.

2

The Thiel Knot

One continuous three-lobed loop with self-crossings. Every lobe stays in the pulsar's sweeping beam and field, and its rectenna skin turns that radiation into current.

3

Tap nodes

Three relay nodes, spaced evenly around the knot, gather the current and beam it out to consumers.

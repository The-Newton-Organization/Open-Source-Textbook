THE WENITTE PYRAMID

A black-hole power station that out-produces a Dyson sphere · side-view schematic (not to scale)

HOW IT WORKS

1

Apex core

A spinning (Kerr) black hole of a few solar masses. Its spin is the battery: the Penrose process and superradiance pull energy from the rotation.

2

Feed line

Asteroids, gas giants or a captured star, streamed into the disk. Matching the Sun at 10% efficiency needs about 4×1010 kg/s.

3

Collector facets

Four sloped faces at a safe orbital radius catch disk radiation. The pyramid shape keeps them angled off the jet axis.

4

Base tier

Radiators and power-beaming stations send the energy to wherever it is needed.

DYSON SPHERE

Powered by the star it wraps

Sun output: about 3.8×1026 W

Fusion converts about 0.7% of fuel mass

WENITTE PYRAMID

Powered by infalling mass

Same output at 10%: about 4×1010 kg/s

Disk converts 6% (static) to 40% (max spin)

THE CATCH

A black hole converts, not creates

Needs a steady mass supply

Heavy radiation shielding required
